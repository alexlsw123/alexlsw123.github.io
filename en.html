Four forecast problems caught before launch

All four surfaced while replaying production. Struck-through numbers are the values before the fix.

LH-024 / 9

01

01Stopped leakage in the training data

Gap filling looked both ways, so values from after the cutoff leaked into inputs and offline accuracy ran 17% above the production replay.

  • 6.4% of cells in the last 24 input hours differed from production
  • Panels rebuilt for every date from raw data up to that hour
  • 31,377 samples, 40 min on 4 processes

before 0.8700.873

R², production replay on 42 complexes. In spring it went from trailing HGB to beating it.

02

02Filters meter spikes before forecasting

One 15-minute reading about 1,290× normal stayed in the inputs for 14 days, scaling blew up, and one complex's R² fell to −881.

  • Scaling by median and IQR instead of mean and SD
  • Clipped at ±8, capped at 6× the input median before publishing
  • No forecast published beyond 10× a complex's usual level

before −19.260.781

January mean R² of the networks. Gating on the mean, not the median, caught it before launch.

03

03Forecasts late meter data too

Meter data arrives at different times per complex; with one cutoff for all, the 2026-08-26 replay forecast only 29 of 77.

  • Delay of 0–12 h as an input, randomized in training
  • Histories under 14 days padded by repeating the days on hand
  • Only complexes silent for a month are skipped; fixes logged in the manifest

before 74.1%77.4%

Coverage. Most complexes still skipped have no ground truth either.

Tariff-tier alerts: will this month pass 400 kWh?

The forecasting model I deployed for a Homeniq feature that warns Raemian Oneperla households when this month's use will reach the 400 kWh tariff tier. A new complex has no history at move-in, so the model changes in three phases as data accumulates.

HN-016 / 9

  1. GBDT on a similar complexPhase I · at move-inUnit area, floor, weekday and month cycles, last-3-day mean, solar radiation, holidays
  2. DeepAR on a month of historyPhase II · after two monthsForecasts the month by drawing on similar learned patterns
  3. Household ID embeddingsPhase III · after a yearLearns each household's own cycles from a year of records

4-bit quantization safety paper

Do NVIDIA's 4-bit NVFP4 checkpoints refuse the same harmful requests as the originals? I measured it on 2,014 paired English and Korean requests.

R-017 / 9

  • NVFP4 flips 5–10% of harmful requests from refusal to compliance (rerun noise 0.5–2.2%)
  • Korean refusals flip 3–9 pp more often in the widest-gap model
  • Splitting the same checkpoint across two GPUs flips up to 9% of Korean harmful requests
  • Submitted to the AAAI-27 Deployable AI workshop · first author
